Who We Are

Protecting Forests by Empowering the People Who Depend on Them

Canopy Foundation Tanzania is a national NGO working across Mainland Tanzania and Zanzibar. We focus on communities living alongside degraded forests, supporting them with conservation awareness, sustainable income opportunities, and community-led restoration.

01 Community Empowerment
02 Forest Restoration
03 Sustainable Livelihoods

Vision

A Tanzania where forest-adjacent communities are empowered stewards of healthy ecosystems, enjoying improved livelihoods and a climate-resilient future.

Mission

To empower communities living adjacent to degraded forests through conservation awareness, sustainable income-generating opportunities, and active community-led restoration.

What We Do

Turning Community Action Into Lasting Environmental Change

🌱

Community Awareness & Education

Building environmental knowledge and encouraging positive conservation behavior among communities living near threatened and degraded forests.

🌳

Forest Restoration

Mobilizing, training, and equipping communities to restore degraded forests through tree planting, nature-based solutions, and assisted natural regeneration.

🐝

Green Livelihoods

Supporting sustainable income opportunities including agroforestry, beekeeping, eco-tourism, and sustainable non-timber forest products.

🛰️

Research & Innovation

Using scientific research, GIS technology, and policy advocacy to strengthen community-led conservation and long-term restoration.
